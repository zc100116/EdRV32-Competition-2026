# File: edrv32_fpga_npu_top.fdc
# Top: edrv32_fpga_npu_top (npu-plan N-W2).
# Measurement only, not run on the board. clk_npu_i is the 27 MHz board clock on D18,
# constrained at the clk_npu development target 150 MHz (npu-spec 9 item 1); clk_mem_i
# enters on K21, the clock-capable HDMI-in pixel clock pin of the vendor HDMI demos
# (B24 was rejected by PnR Place-0084 in 20261028-01), and is constrained at
# 125 MHz (SoC spec 5.1). The two clocks are asynchronous (SoC spec 5.1); their only
# crossings are the APB/AXI256 asynchronous bridges inside edrv32_npu (SoC 5.2 X9, X10).
# Other pins reuse edrv32_fpga_cpu_sys_top.fdc: rst_n_i on key C22, stim_i on key C23,
# obs_o on LED A20, irq_obs_o on LED C18.
