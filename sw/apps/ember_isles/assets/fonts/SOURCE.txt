The Ember UI subset adds one original native 12px glyph: U+2212 mathematical
minus, advance 6px, four opaque pixels on row 5. It is declared explicitly in
font_convert.py and dedicated to CC0 by this project. No missing character is
replaced automatically. Fusion Pixel does not include this required symbol.
